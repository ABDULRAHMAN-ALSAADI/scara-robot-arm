## Mechanical mechanisms

### Z-axis components

![Z-axis components](../media/mechanical/z-axis-components.jpg)

The vertical mechanism uses a lead screw for lifting and 10 mm steel shafts with LM10UU linear bearings for structural guidance.

### L1 to L2 belt transmission

![L1 to L2 transmission](../media/mechanical/l1-l2-belt-transmission.jpg)

The first arm transmission uses two GT2 belt stages and a compound pulley arrangement to increase torque and reduce backlash.

### L2 to L3 belt transmission

![L2 to L3 transmission](../media/mechanical/l2-l3-belt-transmission.jpg)

The third-axis motor is placed close to Joint 2 to reduce swing inertia, with belt transmission carrying motion to Joint 3.

## Electronics

![Electronics wiring diagram](../media/electronics/electronics-wiring-diagram.jpg)

The control system combines an ESP32, DRV8825 drivers, NEMA 17 stepper motors, a servo gripper, limit switches, a 12 V motor-power rail, and a regulated low-voltage rail.
